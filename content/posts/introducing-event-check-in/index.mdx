Registration is the first thing every guest sees, and usually the part with the least software behind it: a printed list, a highlighter and a queue that stretches into the street. Today we are launching **CueDeck Event Check-in** to fix that.

Check-in runs the registration desk for one event, from the guest list to the report afterwards. It works on its own or alongside the CueDeck console you already use for the show, with the same login and the same events.

![The check-in desk: one search brings up three guests from the same company](/screenshots/checkin-desk-group-arrival.jpg)

## Built for the twenty minutes when everyone arrives

Most check-in tools treat every guest as a separate scan. In practice people arrive in groups: a team from the same company, a speaker with a colleague. On the CueDeck desk, one scan or search brings up everyone from that company. Tick who is standing there and check them in together.

- **Scan or search.** A USB QR scanner, or two letters of a name, company or email.
- **Badges on demand.** Badges print through Chrome to any printer the laptop can use. No print server.
- **Walk-ins.** Add them at the desk, or let them register themselves on a paired kiosk tablet.
- **Mistakes are undoable.** Every correction is kept in the log; nothing is erased.
- **Works offline.** If the venue Wi-Fi drops, the desk keeps going and syncs when it is back.

## Any phone is a door scanner

Pair a phone from the desk, choose its door, and it checks people in without a desk at all. It confirms each guest with a sound and a clear tick, tells you when someone is already in, and refuses codes from other events. The phone keeps only the list of codes, never names, so a phone left on a chair gives nothing away.

![A door scanner phone confirming a checked-in guest](/screenshots/checkin-door-scanner-phone.jpg)

## See the doors from anywhere

The live dashboard shows arrivals every fifteen minutes, every desk and whether it is synced, and a company board with who is here, who is partly here and who has not arrived. Choose ticket types such as VIP or Speaker, and the dashboard and desk leads see an alert the moment one of them checks in.

![The live check-in dashboard with arrivals, desks, alerts and the company board](/screenshots/checkin-dashboard-live.jpg)

## The report writes itself

When check-in closes, the event owner gets a report by email: turnout by ticket type, the busiest fifteen minutes, check-ins per desk, anything that was checked in offline and synced later, and the companies that had people missing.

![The post-event check-in report](/screenshots/checkin-post-event-report.jpg)

## Set up in an afternoon

- Import your guest list from a CSV. CueDeck shows who will be added, updated or skipped before anything is saved.
- Invite your team by email as organizers, desk leads, crew or viewers. They only ever see your event.
- Rehearse in test mode: everything works, with up to 25 test check-ins that are cleared when you go live.
- Go live, then send every guest an email with their personal QR code.

The full walkthrough is in the docs: [Run check-in for your first event](/docs#check-in-first-event).
